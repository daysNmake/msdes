# DMT costs by year

A matrix of annual Disease Modifying Therapy DMT costs by year

## Usage

``` r
df_c_tx
```

## Format

A matrix with `3` rows and `17` columns. Each column contains the costs
by treatment:

- cost_annual_yr1:

  Annual DMT costs during 1st year of treatment

- cost_annual_yr2:

  Annual DMT costs during 2nd year of treatment

## Source

British National Formulary
