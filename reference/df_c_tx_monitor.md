# Treatment monitoring costs by year

A matrix of annual treatment monitoring costs by year

## Usage

``` r
df_c_tx_monitor
```

## Format

A matrix with `3` rows and `17` columns. Each column contains the costs
by treatment:

- cost_annual_yr1:

  Annual treatment monitoring costs during 1st year of treatment

- cost_annual_yr2:

  Annual treatment monitoring costs during 2nd year of treatment

## Source

UoB_TAG analysis (refer to report)
