# A matrix of patient counts stratified by treatment

A matrix of patient counts stratified by treatment

## Usage

``` r
m_proportion_ontrtline
```

## Format

A matrix with `4` rows and `8` columns. Each column contains the number
of patients on treatment at line of treatment :

- alpha_2ndline:

  distribution of patients on 2nd line treatment

- beta_2ndline:

  distribution of patients on 2nd line treatment

- alpha_3rdline:

  distribution of patients on 3rd line treatment

- beta_3rdline:

  distribution of patients on 3rd line treatment
