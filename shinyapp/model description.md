### Model Overview
The DES model simulates Individual patients’ events conditional on individual attributes updated in continuous time.
10 The simulated individual patient attributes represent important patient, disease and treatment characteristics. 
Attributes at baseline are HARRMS patients with average age of 36 years, 70% female and severity distribution according to MS-Registry.

The model simulates Individual patient trajectories by randomly drawing event times for the following events; 
severity progression & lessening, relapse, adverse events, treatment switching, conversion to SPMS and death. 
The model is illustrated in figure 1. Simulated patients start in the model on a random initial treatment, 
they have to ability to switch on to later line treatment and rescue therapy as recommended by the guideline. 

Treatment sequence is represented in figure 2. Costs and effects are added for treatments on an annual basis, 
for events instantaneously, and a 3.5% discount factor is applied. The analysis was conducted from National Health Service (NHS) 
and Personal Social Services (PSS) perspective with a life-time horizon.
